About

I am a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Salamanca working on neutron stars and dense nuclear matter. My research focuses on connecting microphysical models of dense matter with multimessenger astrophysical observations.

My work combines relativistic mean field theory, Bayesian inference, and gravitational wave observations to understand the equation of state of dense matter.

Research

Main Topics:

  • Neutron star equation of state
  • Relativistic mean field theory
  • Hyperons and quark matter
  • Gravitational waves and multimessenger astronomy
  • Bayesian inference in astrophysics

I develop theoretical models of dense matter and confront them with observations from NICER and LIGO-Virgo-KAGRA collaborations.
